What Is a Printable Polycarbonate Sheet?

Printable polycarbonate sheets are thermoplastic films made from optical-grade polycarbonate. They are screen printed, die-cut or laser-cut and then formed into overlays and windows for finished products. People choose it for clarity, impact resistance and the ability to take a shape without losing its look. The two POLYVANTIS grades stocked by Screensource are:

  • 8010 Clear Film: A polished, double-sided optical film with high light transmission and good dimensional heat stability. It suits LED displays, touchscreens and illuminated dials.
  • 8B35 Velvet Matte Film: Velvet on one side and fine matte on the other. The surface scatters reflected light and protects printed graphics from finger abrasion.

Applications of Printable Polycarbonate Sheets

Printable polycarbonate suits any printed surface that has to look good and survive daily handling. Typical uses include:

  • Membrane Switches and Keypads: The velvet matte film feels pleasant under the finger, and it resists the repeated pressing that wears away lesser prints.
  • Control Panels and Graphic Overlays: Machine panels and equipment fascias need clear, lasting labels, and polycarbonate holds fine print detail through daily knocks.
  • Automotive Dials and Instrument Clusters: Dashboard instruments need clarity and heat tolerance, which the polished clear film provides for illuminated dials and backlit graphics.
  • Medical Equipment Overlays: Surfaces on medical devices are cleaned often, and the film's resistance to household cleaners and solvents is useful here.
  • Home Appliances: Washing machines, microwaves and other appliances carry printed control overlays that need to stay sharp for years.
  • Backlit Signage and Displays: High light transmission helps illuminated panels glow evenly, and both the clear and textured finishes suit this work.

Salient Features of Printable Polycarbonate Sheets

The POLYVANTIS films we supply combine optical and mechanical properties:

  • High Optical Clarity: The double-polished 8010 film is crystal clear, so light passes through cleanly and LED displays look their best.
  • Printable and Formable: The film accepts solvent and UV screen inks and can be thermoformed, so one material covers flat overlays and deep-drawn parts.
  • Heat and Flame Resistance: A high glass transition temperature lets the film handle demanding processing and warm operating conditions.
  • Anti-Glare Matte Surface: The 8B35 texture diffuses reflected light, so graphics stay readable in bright environments.
  • Scratch and Chemical Resistance: The matte film resists scuffing, household cleaners and solvents, so printed surfaces look presentable for longer.
  • Die-Cutting Precision: Excellent gauge uniformity allows clean die-cutting, embossing and laser processing, and finished parts come out neat.

Benefits of Printable Polycarbonate Sheets
