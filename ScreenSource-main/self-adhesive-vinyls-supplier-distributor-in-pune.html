What Are Self Adhesive Vinyls?

Self adhesive vinyls are thin plastic films with a pressure-sensitive adhesive on the back and a release liner that peels away. You cut or print the graphic, remove the liner and press it onto the surface. No glue, paint or screws are needed, which is why vinyl suits both one-off signs and large fleet jobs.

The vinyls we supply come in three main types:

  • Plotter and cut vinyls: Coloured films cut by a plotter into lettering, logos and shapes.
  • Digital print vinyls: White or clear films made to be printed on solvent, eco-solvent, UV or latex printers.
  • Cast wrapping films: Thin, conformable films for full or partial vehicle wraps.

Applications Of Self Adhesive Vinyls

Self adhesive vinyls are used wherever a graphic must be fixed quickly and look sharp. Common uses include:

  • Vehicle and fleet wraps: Cast wrapping films follow curves, rivets and corrugations on cars, vans and buses.
  • Shop signage and lettering: Plotter vinyls give clean, cut lettering for fascias, doors and windows.
  • Window and glass graphics: Printable and clear films decorate shop fronts and glass partitions.
  • Backlit signs and lightboxes: Translucent vinyls glow evenly for channel letters and display boxes.
  • Industrial labels and decals: Print vinyls carry safety markings and equipment warnings.
  • Marine and transport branding: Seawater-resistant cast films suit boats and public transport.

Each job has its own surface and durability needs, so we help you check them before you order.

Salient Features Of Self Adhesive Vinyls

Good self adhesive vinyl combines grip, durability and easy handling. Here is how the Orafol, Avery and 3M products measure up:

  • Strong, lasting adhesion: ORACAL 751C and 640 use permanent solvent-polyacrylate adhesives that hold on vehicles, metal and glass, while removable grades such as ORACAL 620 come off cleanly.
  • Outdoor durability: ORACAL 970 offers up to 12 years, ORACAL 751C up to 8 years, and Avery signage vinyls an 8 to 10 year outdoor life.
  • Bubble-free application: ORACAL 970 uses RapidAir® channels and Avery uses Easy Apply RS air channels, so trapped air escapes under the squeegee.
  • Conformability: Cast films such as ORACAL 751C and Avery Supreme Wrapping Vinyl follow rivers, corrugations and complex curves.
  • Colour and finish choice: ORACAL 970 has over 130 colours, ORACAL 641 has 60 gloss and matt shades, and Avery adds satin, metallic, pearl and brushed finishes.
  • Printable and cut-ready: ORAJET films print on solvent, eco-solvent, UV and latex machines, and ORACAL 641 and Avery 700 cut film suit plotter work.
  • Marine and fuel resistance: ORACAL 751C resists seawater and fuel, and ORACAL 640 carries BS 5609 marine certification.
  • Application support: ORATAPE LT95 and MT95 transfer cut lettering without residue, and 3M 468MP attaches graphics at up to 204°C.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What are self adhesive vinyls?

Q2. Which ORACAL vinyls do you supply?

Q3. What is the difference between cast and calendered vinyl?

Cast films are thinner and more conformable, so they suit vehicle curves and rivets. Calendered films are usually economical choices for flat or gently curved signage.
