What Are VHB & UHB Tapes?

VHB & UHB Tapes are double-sided pressure-sensitive tapes built on a soft acrylic foam core. VHB stands for Very High Bond and UHB for Ultra High Bond, and both are designed to join metal, glass and plastic with a strong, flexible and often invisible bond.

The range we stock includes three well-known tapes:

  • ORABOND® UHB03100T: A clear 40-mil (about 1 mm) foamed acrylic tape with a crystal-clear bondline. It suits illuminated displays, signage, safety glass and shower screens.
  • ORABOND® UHB04064G: A grey 25-mil (about 0.64 mm) foamed acrylic tape for structural bonding. It is used on automotive trims, solar panels, electronics and industrial signs.
  • 3M VHB acrylic foam tapes: Very High Bond double-sided foam tapes that remove the need for screws, rivets and spot welds. They absorb thermal expansion and bond aluminium, stainless steel, glass and composites.

Applications Of VHB & UHB Tapes

Foam tapes are used wherever a strong, neat bond is needed without drilling or welding. Common uses include:

  • Illuminated signage and displays: ORABOND® UHB03100T gives a crystal-clear bondline, so lightboxes and displays look clean from the front.
  • Glass and shower screens: The clear foam core suits safety glass and structural transparent joints where the adhesive must stay unseen.
  • Automotive trims and mirrors: UHB04064G bonds trims, and ORABOND® 1397TM suits mirrors and decorative strips on rough surfaces.
  • Solar panels and electronics: The grey foam tape suits solar panel mounting and electronic assemblies that face daily temperature changes.
  • Metal and signage fabrication: 3M VHB tapes replace screws, rivets and spot welds on aluminium and stainless steel panels, leaving a smooth finish.
  • Nameplates and membrane switches: 3M transfer tapes 467MP and 468MP suit nameplates, graphic attachment and membrane switch work.

Salient Features Of VHB & UHB Tapes

The tapes we supply offer a practical mix of strength, flexibility and clarity:

  • Foamed acrylic core: The elastomeric core compensates for thermal expansion, up to 300% on UHB03100T, which helps bonds survive heat and cold.
  • High shear and tensile strength: ORABOND® UHB04064G lists 132 lb/in² dynamic shear and 142 lb/in² tensile strength.
  • Wide temperature range: UHB04064G is listed from -40°F to +212°F, with a maximum of 320°F.
  • Splice Free Guarantee: Both ORABOND® UHB tapes carry a Splice Free Guarantee for reliable, continuous rolls.
  • Weather and solvent resistance: 3M VHB tapes resist moisture, extreme temperatures, solar UV light and solvents.
  • Multi-substrate adhesion: The range bonds aluminium, stainless steel, glass, composites and high surface energy plastics.

Benefits Of VHB & UHB Tapes

Choosing the right foam tape pays off at every stage of fabrication and use:

  • Cleaner looking products: No visible screws, rivets or weld marks keep signs, panels and glass neat.
  • Even stress distribution: A viscoelastic foam bond spreads load across the joint and dampens vibration.
  • Faster assembly: Peel, press and bond replaces drilling, fastening or welding, which saves labour time.
  • Longer lasting joints: Weather, UV and temperature resistance keep bonds secure outdoors.
  • Easier sourcing: Tapes, films and graphic products can come from one team. You spend less time chasing different vendors.
