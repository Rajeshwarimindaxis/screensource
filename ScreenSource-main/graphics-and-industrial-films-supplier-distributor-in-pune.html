What Are Graphics & Industrial Films?

Graphics & Industrial Films are engineered rolls and sheets of vinyl, polyester and specialty materials, designed to be printed, cut, laminated and fitted into signs, vehicles and finished products. They are valued for outdoor durability, colour quality and the ability to decorate, protect or bond a surface.

The range we stock includes three main groups:

  • Graphic vinyls and print films: Includes ORACAL® vinyls and ORAJET® digital media for signage, vehicle wraps and window graphics. They suit solvent, eco-solvent, UV and latex printing.
  • Laminating and reflective films: Here, ORAGUARD® overlaminates add UV protection to printed graphics, while ORALITE® sheeting gives high visibility on traffic and safety signs.
  • Industrial films and tapes: MacDermid hard coated films, 3M tapes and TechNova NovaLife media serve membrane switches, labels and displays. ORABOND® tapes handle bonding and mounting.

Applications of Graphics & Industrial Films

  • Vehicle and fleet wraps: ORACAL® 970 Premium Wrapping Cast uses RapidAir® channels for bubble-free application over curves.
  • Shopfronts and signage: ORAJET® 3162 suits window graphics, and the optically clear ORAJET® 3452E works on glass partitions.
  • Wall murals and floors: ORAJET® wall films decorate interiors, and anti-slip ORAGUARD® 250AS protects printed floor graphics in retail aisles.
  • Traffic and road safety: ORALITE® reflective sheeting meets CE and EN 12899-1 standards for highway, city and work-zone signs.
  • Membrane switches and keypads: MacDermid hard coated films, the 3M 7945 MP membrane switch spacer and 3M SP7533 screen-printable adhesive all serve this work.
  • Bonding and mounting: ORABOND® 1375 transfer tape, 3M VHB tapes and ORAFLEX® mounting tape fix signs, front plates and printing plates securely.
  • Backlit displays, tags and menus: TechNova NovaLife backlit PET and synthetic papers suit lightboxes, outdoor tags and menus.

Salient Features of Graphics & Industrial Films

  • Long outdoor life: Premium cast ORAFOL films such as ORAJET® 3951 are rated for up to 10 years outdoors, and ORACAL® 970 is listed with 12-year durability.
  • Recognised certifications: The range carries marks such as BS 5609 marine, DIN EN 13501-1 fire behaviour, UL 969 and EN 12899-1.
  • Heat and chemical resistance: ORABOND® 1375 handles -40°C to +170°C, and 3M 467MP and 468MP tapes cope with temperatures up to 204°C.
  • Tough, printable surfaces: MacDermid hard coated films resist scratches and chemicals, while TechNova NovaLife PET films are waterproof and tear-proof.
  • Special surfaces: ORAGUARD® 289F has a self-healing surface, and ORAGUARD® 244G offers anti-graffiti protection. Both are built for long-term outdoor use.
