Technical Specifications

3M Product Range

High-performance industrial adhesive tapes engineered to replace mechanical fasteners with long-lasting structural bond strength.

3M VHB Acrylic Foam Tapes

Very High Bond double-sided foam tapes engineered to eliminate screws, rivets, and spot welds while absorbing thermal expansion.

  • High-Strength Structural Bonding: Creates a permanent viscoelastic seal that dampens vibration and distributes stress evenly.
  • Multi-Substrate Adhesion: Bonds aluminum, stainless steel, glass, composite materials, and high surface energy plastics.
  • Weather & Environmental Resistance: Complete resistance to moisture, extreme temperatures, solar UV light, and solvents.

Double-Coated & Transfer Tapes

Precision thin-profile adhesive solutions for graphics, nameplates, membrane switches, and electronic assembly applications.

  • Adhesive Transfer Tapes: Solvent-free pure acrylic adhesive films providing high shear strength and initial tack.
  • Tissue & Film Carrier Tapes: Double-sided tapes (such as 3M 91091) featuring dimensionally stable tissue carriers for easy die-cutting.
  • Residue-Free Clean Peel: Specialized removable adhesives ideal for temporary holding and precision alignment.

Specialty & Industrial Tapes

Engineered performance tapes for surface protection, insulation, high-temperature masking, and heavy-duty bundling.

  • High Temperature Masking: Crepe and polyimide tapes withstand extreme baking cycles without adhesive residue.
  • Surface Protection Films: Guards delicate metal sheets, acrylic panels, and glass against scratch damage during transit.
  • Electrical & Anti-Slip Solutions: Heavy-duty vinyl electrical insulation tapes and Safety-Walk anti-slip treads.
