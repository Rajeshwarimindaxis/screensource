Premium Aluminum Frames

Our high-quality aluminum screen printing frames provide the perfect balance of lightweight maneuverability and rigid durability for flawless prints.

  • Lightweight & Durable: Built to withstand heavy use without warping.
  • Optimal Tension: Welded construction ensures consistent mesh tension.
  • Water-Tight Seals: Prevents ink and chemical leakage during printing.
  • Multiple Sizes: Available in various dimensions to suit any project.
  • Corrosion Resistant: Long-lasting performance even after repeated washing.

MacDermid Alpha

Industry-leading screen printing chemicals and inks offering unparalleled consistency, performance, and vibrant results for all your professional applications.

  • High Performance: Formulated for exceptional print clarity and edge definition.
  • Consistent Viscosity: Ensures smooth application and reliable production runs.
  • Fast Curing: Optimized for quick drying times to increase efficiency.
  • Vibrant Colors: Delivers outstanding color accuracy and opacity.
  • Eco-Friendly Options: Available in low-VOC formulations for safer workspaces.

Premium Lamination Films

Protect and enhance your printed materials with our high-grade lamination films. Engineered for superior clarity, durability, and a flawless finish.

  • Crystal Clear Finish: Enhances color depth and contrast of underlying prints.
  • Scratch Resistant: Provides a robust barrier against daily wear and tear.
  • Moisture Protection: Seals out water and humidity to prolong print life.
  • Easy Application: Designed for smooth, bubble-free adhesion.
  • Multiple Finishes: Available in gloss, matte, and soft-touch textures.

Premium Polyester Films

Achieve the highest quality screen positives with our premium polyester films, engineered for optimal ink adhesion and exceptional dimensional stability.

  • High Density: Guarantees deep, opaque blacks for perfect UV blocking.
  • Fast Drying: Specially coated to absorb ink rapidly without smudging.
  • Dimensional Stability: Will not warp or shrink, ensuring perfect registration.
  • Excellent Clarity: Highly transparent base for precise stencil creation.
  • Universal Compatibility: Works perfectly with most inkjet printers and inks.

Premium Indoor / Outdoor Graphic Solutions, Adhesive Tapes, Traffic & Infrastructure

Comprehensive industrial adhesive tapes, signage vinyls, and reflective media from ORAFOL Germany, engineered for extreme weather durability, high-tack bonding, and traffic safety compliance.

  • ORABOND® Industrial Tapes: High-performance double-sided transfer and bonding tapes with extreme shear strength.
  • ORACAL® Graphic Vinyls: Weatherproof cast and calendered colored films for long-lasting signage and vehicle branding.
  • ORAJET® Digital Media: High-definition printable films compatible with solvent, eco-solvent, UV, and latex printers.
  • Traffic & Infrastructure: Heavy-duty reflective sheeting (ORALITE®) certified for roadway safety and architectural guidance.
  • Exceptional Versatility: Bonds securely across diverse metal, acrylic, glass, and low-surface-energy substrates.
