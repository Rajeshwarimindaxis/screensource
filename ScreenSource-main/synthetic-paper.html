Material Performance

Technical Key Features

Engineered to replace fragile paper with water-proof, tear-resistant, and chemically inert synthetic substrates.

100% Waterproof & Weatherproof

Resists water, moisture, grease, oil, and liquid spills completely, eliminating the need for thermal lamination.

  • Lamination-Free Durability: Eliminates lamination edge delamination, curling, and extra finishing costs.
  • Wipeable & Washable: Easily cleaned with damp cloth or sanitizers without affecting print quality.
  • UV & Outdoor Resistance: Maintains bright white appearance without yellowing under sunlight.

Universal Digital & Offset Printability

Both-side matte treated surface accepts laser toner, HP Indigo, offset inks, flexo, and screen printing inks with high clarity.

  • High Toner Anchorage: Prevents flaking, scratching, and ghosting during digital printing cycles.
  • Exceptional Flatness: Stiff PET core lays flat for smooth feeding in high-speed digital presses.
  • Vibrant Color Reproduction: Bright white base enhances contrast for crisp text and vivid graphics.

Versatile Commercial Applications

Ideal for demanding applications that require long life under harsh physical handling and outdoor exposure.

  • Restaurant & Hotel Menus: Washable, tear-free menus that withstand daily customer usage.
  • ID Cards & Certificates: Durable identification cards, membership cards, and permanent certificates.
  • Industrial Tags & Manuals: Machinery operating instructions, warning tags, and outdoor plant labels.
