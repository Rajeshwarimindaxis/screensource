Polyester Mesh – The screen printing mesh is the foundation of every print stencil. High-tension monofilament polyester mesh ensures precise ink deposit, dimensional registration accuracy, and long stencil life under heavy squeegee pressure.

Product Information

Screen Source is the authorized distributor of Nittoku, the premier Japanese manufacturer of high-precision screen printing mesh, in Narhe, Pune. We stock white and dyed yellow monofilament mesh spanning 15T to 180T mesh counts.

Yellow mesh eliminates light scatter during UV stencil exposure, enabling microscopic halftones and razor-sharp line definition. Combined with our pneumatic screen stretching facility, we deliver screens with uniform tension up to 35-50 N/cm.

Grades & Specifications

  • Fine Count Yellow Mesh (90T - 165T) (High-Resolution UV Printing): Zero light scattering during UV exposure. Essential for 4-color process halftones, PCB lines, and optical overlays.
  • Medium Count Mesh (43T - 77T) (General Graphics & Transfers): Balanced ink deposit and sharp edge definition for signs, stickers, industrial dials, and decorative panels.
  • Coarse Mesh (15T - 36T) (Heavy Ink Deposit & Specialty Inks): Wide open area for metallic pigments, glitter inks, ceramic enamels, and heavy adhesive coatings.

Typical Applications

  • Printed Electronics & PCB: High-precision solder mask printing, legend printing, and conductive silver ink deposit.
  • Automotive Dials & Clusters: Uniform ink density and micro-fine lines for backlit speedometer graphics and warning icons.
  • Glass & Touchscreen Displays: Scratch-resistant ceramic ink printing on automotive glass and smartphone touch lenses.
  • Photovoltaic & Solar Cells: Ultra-high tension mesh for conductive finger printing on silicon wafers.
  • Textile & Garment Printing: High-speed screen printing for plastisol, water-based, and discharge fashion inks.
  • Industrial Nameplates & Labels: Sharp edge definition on aluminium, stainless steel, and plastic rating labels.
