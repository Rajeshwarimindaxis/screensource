What Are Transfer Tapes?

A transfer tape is a layer of pressure-sensitive adhesive on a release liner, with no film carrier in the middle. You press the adhesive onto one part, peel away the liner, and the adhesive is left behind, ready to join the second surface. Because the layer is so thin, the joint looks neat and almost invisible.

ORABOND® 1375 is built this way:

  • Adhesive: A 60-micron, highly transparent pure acrylic layer.
  • Liner: A double-sided siliconised PE paper liner of 100 g/m².
  • Formats: Roll (1375) or sheet (1375 S), with the sheet liner carrying the blue ORAFOL logo.

Applications of Transfer Tapes

Transfer tapes are used wherever a slim, strong and tidy bond is needed. In sign making, printing and light manufacturing, the typical uses include:

  • Signs and front plates: Fixing metal and acrylic signage and front plates without screws or visible glue.
  • Nameplates and badges: Holding metal nameplates flat and flush on machines and equipment.
  • Displays: Bonding display panels and illuminated displays where a clear bondline matters.
  • Varnished surfaces: Joining parts to varnished or coated surfaces that other tapes may not grip well.
  • Graphic overlays: Mounting printed overlays and panels so the edges stay down.
  • Heat-exposed assemblies: Parts near warm components, where a weaker adhesive would soften and let go.

In each case the surface and the working conditions decide the result, so it helps to check them before you order. Our team can look at your job and confirm whether ORABOND 1375 is the right match.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is ORABOND 1375 transfer tape?

It is a 60-micron, highly transparent pure acrylic transfer tape on a siliconised PE paper liner. It gives a permanent bond with extreme shear strength, and it is UL 969 certified.

Q2. How is a transfer tape different from a double-sided tape?

A transfer tape is adhesive only, with no carrier film, so the layer is thinner and clearer. A double-sided tape such as ORABOND 1331 has a polyester film carrier with adhesive on both sides.

Q3. Which surfaces can it bond?

ORABOND 1375 is made for metals, varnishes, front plates and displays. For low-energy plastics, please ask us, because a tissue or foam tape may suit better.

Q4. What temperatures can it handle?

It works from -40°C to +170°C, with short exposure up to 200°C. Please check the data sheet for the full limits.

Q5. Is it available in sheets, and is a data sheet available?

Yes. ORABOND 1375 S is the sheet version, and ORABOND 1375 is the roll. Technical data sheets for both can be downloaded from our Orafol page.
