Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our screen stretching services.

What is screen stretching?

Screen stretching is the process of applying controlled tension to printing mesh and securely fixing it to a frame.

Why is uniform tension important?

Uniform tension improves print consistency, registration accuracy and screen performance.

Which mesh can you stretch?

We handle polyester and industrial printing meshes suitable for various printing applications.

Can Screensource help select the right mesh?

Yes. Our technical team can recommend suitable mesh specifications based on the printing application.

Do you provide screen stretching for industrial applications?

Yes. We provide screen stretching services for electronics, automotive, membrane, glass, ceramic, industrial graphics and other specialty applications.

Need Professional Screen Stretching?

Send us your frame size, mesh specification and application details. Our technical team will help you select the appropriate mesh and stretching solution.
