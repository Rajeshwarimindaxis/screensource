What Are Adhesive Tapes?

Adhesive tapes are rolls or sheets of a carrier material such as film, tissue or foam, coated with a pressure-sensitive adhesive. They bond surfaces under light pressure, with no drilling, welding or liquid glue. Industrial grades are made for specific jobs like bonding, mounting, splicing, masking and surface protection.

The range we stock includes:

  • Double-sided and transfer tapes: Adhesive on both sides, or a pure adhesive film, for fixing nameplates, front plates and overlays. 3M 467MP and 468MP and ORABOND 1375 are examples.
  • Foam tapes: Acrylic foam tapes such as 3M VHB and ORABOND UHB absorb vibration and thermal expansion, and can replace screws, rivets or spot welds.
  • Tissue tapes: Thin, flexible tapes for mounting screens, foam and felt, available from 3M, Nitto and Sansui.
  • Specialty tapes: High-temperature polyester masking tapes, repulpable splicing tapes, residue-free protection films and application tapes.

Applications of Adhesive Tapes

Adhesive tapes are used wherever parts, graphics or films must be held securely and neatly. Common uses include:

  • Membrane switches and keypads: Transfer and spacer tapes join the graphic overlay and circuit layers, and keep them stable through repeated pressing.
  • Nameplates and graphic overlays: Double-sided and transfer tapes fix metal plates, front panels and printed overlays to machines and equipment.
  • Screen printing prepress: Tissue tapes help mount screens, secure substrate borders, seal frame edges and hold graphics in place.
  • Automotive and appliances: Trims, decorative profiles and foam, felt or gasket parts are laminated and fixed with high-tack tapes.
  • Glass, signage and displays: Clear foam tapes bond illuminated displays, signs and glass panels with a neat, invisible bondline.
  • Paper converting and heat processes: Repulpable tapes splice paper reels at speed, and polyester tapes mask parts in powder-coating and anodizing ovens.

Salient Features of Adhesive Tapes

The tapes we supply offer a practical mix of strength, flexibility and resistance:

  • Strong tack and shear strength: High-performance acrylic adhesives grip metals and many plastics firmly and hold under load.
  • Heat resistance: 3M 467MP and 468MP withstand up to 204°C, and ORABOND 1375 works from -40°C to +170°C.
  • Choice of carriers: Film, tissue and foam carriers suit flat, curved, rough or irregular surfaces.
  • Gap filling and stress absorption: Foam tapes cushion differences in thermal expansion and dampen vibration.
  • Clean removal options: Protection films, application tapes and removable-side tapes such as ORABOND 1331 come off without leaving residue.
  • Chemical, UV and moisture resistance: Selected grades stand up to solvents, sunlight and humid conditions.

Benefits of Adhesive Tapes

The right adhesive tape pays off at every stage of production and use:

  • Strong, lasting bonds: Parts stay fixed through heat, vibration and daily handling, so fewer products come back.
  • Cleaner finish: There are no screw heads, rivets or glue marks, which gives a smoother, more professional look.
  • Faster assembly: Tapes apply quickly and need no curing time, which keeps production moving.
  • Less waste: The correct grade reduces failed bonds and reworked parts, and that keeps costs under control.
  • Easier sourcing: Tapes, films, inks and chemicals can come from one team, which saves you time.
