What Are Customized Aluminium Screen Printing Frames?

An aluminium screen printing frame is the rigid rectangle that holds the mesh under tension while the stencil is exposed and the ink is printed. The mesh is only as flat and as tight as the frame beneath it allows.

A customized frame is made to your measurements, up to 80" x 150", rather than picked from a standard list. That is useful when your press bed, drying rack, glass table or artwork doesn’t match common sizes.

Applications of Customized Aluminium Screen Printing Frames

Large and custom frames are useful wherever the print area is bigger or odder than normal. Common uses include:

  • Large-format graphics and signage: Wide banners, panels and posters need a big, flat screen. A custom frame covers the full artwork in one pass.
  • Textile and garment printing: Long fabric runs and large garment panels need an area that standard frames can’t offer. The frame supports a steady, even print across it.
  • Industrial and electronics printing: Large panels and overlays need accurate registration. A rigid frame helps hold it.
  • Glass and ceramic decoration: Big sheets and decals call for a frame matched to the bed or table. A custom size avoids awkward adjustments.
  • Wedding cards and stationery: Printers who run unusual sheet sizes can get a frame made to fit. That saves wasted space and material.
