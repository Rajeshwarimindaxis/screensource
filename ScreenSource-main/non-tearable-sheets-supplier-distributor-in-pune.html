What Are Non Tearable Sheets?

Non-tearable sheets are synthetic papers. They are made on a plastic film base instead of wood pulp, so they don't soak up water and they don't rip the way normal paper does. You still print on them like paper, but they last far longer.

The Technova NovaLife sheets we stock are built like this:

  • Base: A durable, biaxially oriented PET film.
  • Surface: A matte coating on both sides, which gives sharp print contrast and helps toner anchor quickly.
  • Grades: Matte PET double-sided papers in 115, 125, 175 and 200 DS.

Applications of Non Tearable Sheets

Wherever a printed piece has to survive handling, moisture or outdoor use, a non-tear sheet makes sense. Common uses include:

  • Outdoor tags: Tags exposed to rain, sun and rough handling keep their print and their shape.
  • Menus: Wipe-clean pages stand up to spills, grease and daily use.
  • Certificates and ID cards: The sheet resists creasing and tearing, so documents stay presentable.
  • Photo albums: The matte surface looks smart, and the film base resists wear.
  • Industrial labels: Labels face chemicals, grease and physical strain on the shop floor.
  • Manuals and luxury packaging: Heavy-use manuals and premium packaging need a sheet that holds up.

Each of these jobs asks something different of the sheet. A thin tag and a thick menu board do not need the same grade, so it helps to tell us how the piece will be used before you choose.
