Technical Specifications

Sunsui Product Range

Versatile industrial adhesive tapes engineered to deliver strong hold, high shear resistance, and seamless splicing across printing presses.

Double-Sided Tissue Tapes

Non-woven tissue carrier coated with high-tack acrylic adhesive for instant, discreet, and reliable surface bonding.

  • Screen Printing Prepress: Ideal for mounting screens, securing substrate borders, and holding graphics during printing.
  • Automotive & Appliance Foam Lamination: Bonds foam, felt, and rubber gaskets cleanly without thick seams.
  • Film & Foil Splicing: Facilitates smooth web splicing across flexible packaging and paper converting lines.

Repulpable Splicing Tapes

Water-soluble adhesive formulation designed specifically for high-speed continuous paper manufacturing and newsprint splicing.

  • 100% Recyclable: Fully repulpable adhesive system dissolves cleanly during paper repulping without contaminating batches.
  • Flying Web Splicing: High initial tack enables flawless web transitions at maximum press operating speeds.
  • Temperature Resistance: Withstands elevated drying section heat inside high-speed paper machinery.

High-Temp Polyester Tapes

Durable polyester film backing paired with specialized heat-resistant adhesive for extreme industrial bonding and masking.

  • High-Temperature Masking: Excellent resistance to thermal cycles during powder coating and anodizing bake ovens.
  • Decorative Profile Lamination: Bonds decorative trims, aluminum extrusions, and plastic moldings permanently.
  • Heavy-Duty Metal Bonding: High shear strength on raw metals, powder-coated steel, and treated plastic surfaces.
